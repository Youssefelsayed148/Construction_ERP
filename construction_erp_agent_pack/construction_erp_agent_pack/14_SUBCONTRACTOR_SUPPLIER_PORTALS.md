# 14 - Subcontractor and Supplier Portals

## Subcontractor portal
Dashboard:
- awarded packages/scope
- today's assigned work
- latest approved drawings
- inspections needed
- consultant observations
- RFIs/submittals
- executed/approved quantities
- payment application status
- variations
- snags/warranty

Actions:
- acknowledge subcontract/instructions
- submit RFI
- submit submittal
- request WIR/MIR
- submit progress quantities
- upload manpower/equipment
- respond to NCR/observation
- submit variation quotation
- submit payment application

Isolation:
Subcontractor can only see its own packages and commercial values unless explicitly shared.

## Supplier portal
Dashboard:
- open RFQs
- quotation deadlines
- awarded POs
- deliveries due
- inspection/rejection status
- invoices/payment status
- expiring compliance documents

Actions:
- submit quotation
- send clarification
- acknowledge PO
- propose delivery date
- upload delivery/certificates
- submit invoice

Supplier cannot see competing vendor pricing.
