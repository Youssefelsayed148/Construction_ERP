## Sample BOQ
- C35 concrete: 8,000 m3
- reinforcement steel: 1,000 t
- blockwork: 40,000 m2
- plaster: 65,000 m2
- ceramic flooring: 18,500 m2
- electrical cable: 120 km

Allocate across towers/floors.

## Test material recipe
For each 1 m3 reinforced concrete:
- 1.0 m3 C35 concrete
- 125 kg reinforcement
- 1.5 kg binding wire
- 3.8 m2 formwork
- 10 spacers

## Golden scenario
1. Create project from Residential template.
2. Assign team and external participants.
3. Allocate BOQ by floor.
4. Schedule Tower A Floor 1 concrete.
5. Calculate materials and identify shortage.
6. Auto-create draft PR.
7. Create RFQ to 3 suppliers and choose approved quote.
8. Issue PO through authority workflow.
9. Receive partial delivery, create MIR, accept and GRN.
10. Issue materials to Tower A/Floor 1 work package.
11. Record daily work and executed quantity.
12. Raise WIR; consultant approves with comment.
13. Consultant raises photo observation on another item.
14. PM assigns subcontractor; before/after evidence uploaded; consultant closes.
15. Measure/certify progress.
16. Create client certificate/invoice and partial collection.
17. Create subcontractor certificate/payment.
18. Raise variation linked to RFI and approve.
19. Complete punch items and handover asset/warranty package.
20. Validate dashboards and MCP queries for each role.
