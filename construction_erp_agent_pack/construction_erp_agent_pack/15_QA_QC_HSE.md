# 15 - QA/QC and HSE

## QA/QC entities
- ITP
- ITP point
- WIR
- MIR inspection
- inspection checklist
- quality observation
- NCR
- corrective/preventive action
- quality test
- mock-up approval
- calibration record
- certificate register
- punch item

## ITP
Define inspection points by work package/activity with:
- hold/witness/review point
- required documents
- responsible internal party
- consultant responsibility
- acceptance criteria

## WIR
Fields:
- project/location
- work package/activity
- BOQ item
- subcontractor
- inspection date
- latest approved drawings
- method statement
- checklist
- photos

Workflow configurable: Site -> QA/QC -> PM -> Consultant.

Results:
- approved
- approved with comments
- rejected
- reinspect

## MIR
Link to PO, delivery, supplier, material submittal, certificates and GRN. Accepted quantity controls usable stock.

## NCR
Add:
- exact location
- responsible party
- root cause
- corrective action
- preventive action
- cost/schedule impact
- verification
- closure authority
